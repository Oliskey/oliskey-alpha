# 3. Trading risk disclosure (draft)

> DRAFT for a Nigerian capital-markets lawyer. Not legal advice. The final text must be reviewed for compliance with SEC Nigeria
> rules and shown to customers before they connect an exchange or subscribe.

**Read this before using Oliskey AI Trading.**

1. **You can lose money, including all the money you allocate.** Crypto prices can fall fast and far, at any hour.
2. **No strategy is guaranteed.** Test results and practice results are not promises. A strategy that did well before can do badly
   later. Approval of a strategy version means it passed our tests; it is not a promise of profit.
3. **Back-tests and practice trading have limits.** They use past or live prices without real money, so real fills, fees,
   slippage and timing can be worse.
4. **Automatic orders.** Once you allocate money to a strategy, orders are placed for you without asking each time, within the
   limits you set. Pause stops new orders at once; it does not sell what you hold.
5. **Your money stays on the exchange.** Oliskey never holds your money or crypto. That also means the exchange's own risks are
   yours: outages, account restrictions, hacks, insolvency, or changes to its rules and fees. Oliskey cannot get your money back
   from an exchange.
6. **Technical failures.** The app, our servers, the internet or the exchange can fail or be slow. An order can be delayed,
   rejected or filled at a worse price. We check prices are fresh and limit slippage, but cannot remove this risk.
7. **Your API key.** Create a key that can read and trade spot only, never withdraw. Anyone with your key can trade on your
   account. You can disconnect in the app and should also delete the key on the exchange.
8. **Fees reduce results.** Exchange fees, our subscription and our 15% performance fee on new realised profit all reduce what
   you keep.
9. **Taxes are yours.** You are responsible for any tax on your trading.
10. **Rules can change.** Laws on crypto in Nigeria and elsewhere may change and could limit or stop this service.
11. **Only use money you can afford to lose.** If you are unsure, do not use real money, or speak to a licensed adviser.
