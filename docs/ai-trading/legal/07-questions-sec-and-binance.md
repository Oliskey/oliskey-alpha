# 7. Questions for SEC Nigeria and for the Binance broker programme (draft)

> DRAFT. Not legal advice. Questions to put to the regulator (through the lawyer) and to Binance before real-money trading.

## For SEC Nigeria (via the lawyer)
7.1 Under the Investments and Securities Act 2025 and the SEC's digital-asset rules, does placing spot orders on a customer's own
    exchange account through a trade-only API key, without custody, require registration? If so, which category (e.g. digital
    asset operator, fund/portfolio manager, investment adviser, robo-adviser, technology provider)?
7.2 Does charging (a) a monthly strategy subscription and (b) a 15% performance fee change that answer?
7.3 Is offering this to Nigerian residents permitted when the exchange (Binance) is outside Nigeria? Any restriction on directing
    Nigerians to a specific foreign exchange?
7.4 Is there an innovation/sandbox route (e.g. the SEC's incubation programme for digital-asset businesses) suitable for a beta?
7.5 Rules on advertising and showing back-tested, simulated or past performance of strategies.
7.6 Minimum capital, fidelity bond, insurance or key-person requirements for the applicable category.
7.7 Required disclosures, client agreements, complaint handling and record-keeping periods.
7.8 Does a free practice/testnet beta with no real money require registration or approval?
7.9 Treatment of refunds, disputes and unpaid performance-fee invoices.
7.10 Interaction with CBN rules on paying, in Naira, for a service related to crypto assets.
7.11 Tax: VAT on subscriptions and fees; withholding obligations, if any.

## For the Binance broker programme
B.1 Can Oliskey join the broker (link/API) programme as a Nigerian company, and what are the terms and revenue share?
B.2 Does Binance allow third-party automated trading on customer accounts through customer-created keys, and under which
    conditions? Any rate limits or order-tagging requirements for brokers?
B.3 Can the API confirm a key's permissions (no withdrawal, no transfer) for both live and testnet? (Today the live check uses
    the API restrictions endpoint; testnet has no such endpoint.)
B.4 Can the API confirm the account holder's KYC status or name, to match our customer?
B.5 Is Binance's service available to Nigerian residents for spot BTC/USDT and ETH/USDT, and are there restrictions we must
    enforce?
B.6 Requirements for IP whitelisting of our servers and key rotation.
B.7 Data-sharing terms: what Binance shares with us and what we may store.
B.8 Incident process: whom we contact if Binance or our service has an outage affecting customer orders.
